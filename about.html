About FlowMaster

Trusted plumbing excellence since 2009 — built on craftsmanship, integrity, and a relentless commitment to customer satisfaction.

Our Story

From a Single Van to London's Leading Plumber

FlowMaster was founded in 2009 by Master Plumber David Harris, who started the business with a single van, a commitment to quality, and a belief that customers deserved better than the unreliable service that was common in the industry.

Within five years, FlowMaster had grown to a team of 20 certified plumbers serving across Greater London. Today, with over 45 fully-qualified engineers, we complete more than 4,800 jobs per year — maintaining the same personal touch and quality standards that David instilled from day one.

Our growth has always been driven by referrals from satisfied customers — a testament to the quality and reliability that defines everything we do.

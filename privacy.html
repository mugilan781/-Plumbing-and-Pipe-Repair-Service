Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2026 · Effective immediately

Plain English Summary
  • We only collect what we need to provide our services
  • We never sell your personal data to third parties
  • You can request deletion of your data at any time
  • We use industry-standard security to protect your information
  • We comply fully with UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018

1. Who We Are

FlowMaster Plumbing Limited ("FlowMaster", "we", "us", or "our") is the data controller responsible for your personal data. We are registered in England and Wales with company number 07123456, with our registered office at 42 Copper Lane, London EC1A 1BB.

Our Data Protection Officer can be contacted at privacy@flowmaster.com.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

When you contact us, book a service, or use our website forms, we collect:

  • Full name and contact details (phone, email, address)
  • Service requirements and property information
  • Payment information (processed securely via our payment provider)
  • Any communications you send to us

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, we automatically collect certain technical information including your IP address, browser type and version, pages visited, and time spent on our site. This is collected via cookies (see Section 6).

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your personal data for the following purposes:

  • Service delivery — To schedule, confirm, and complete plumbing appointments
  • Customer communications — To respond to enquiries and provide service updates
  • Payment processing — To charge for services rendered
  • Marketing — To send you relevant offers and updates (only with your consent)
  • Legal compliance — To meet our legal and regulatory obligations
  • Service improvement — To analyse how our services are used to improve them

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following lawful bases:

  • Contract performance — Processing necessary to fulfil our service agreement with you
  • Legitimate interests — Running and improving our business, preventing fraud
  • Consent — Where you have explicitly agreed (e.g., marketing emails)
  • Legal obligation — Where required by law (e.g., tax records)

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your data with:

  • Service providers — Cloud hosting, payment processors, and CRM software (all bound by data processing agreements)
  • Professional advisors — Accountants, lawyers, and insurers where necessary
  • Regulatory authorities — When required by law or regulatory process

All third parties we work with are required to maintain appropriate security standards and are prohibited from using your data for their own purposes.

6. Cookies

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies — Required for the website to function (cannot be disabled)
  • Analytics cookies — To understand how visitors use our site (Google Analytics)
  • Preference cookies — To remember your settings (e.g., dark mode, language)
  • Marketing cookies — To deliver relevant advertising (only with consent)

You can manage your cookie preferences through our cookie banner or your browser settings.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal data for the following periods:

  • Customer records — 7 years after last service (for tax and legal compliance)
  • Marketing preferences — Until you withdraw consent
  • Website analytics — 26 months
  • Enquiry forms — 2 years if no service was booked

8. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access — Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Rectification — Ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Erasure — Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Restriction — Ask us to limit how we use your data
  • Portability — Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection — Object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw consent — Withdraw marketing consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@flowmaster.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are unsatisfied with how we handle your request, you have the right to complain to the ICO (Information Commissioner's Office) at ico.org.uk.

9. Security

We implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These include SSL encryption, access controls, and regular security assessments.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you by email. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us:

  • By email: privacy@flowmaster.com
  • By post: Data Protection Officer, FlowMaster Plumbing, 42 Copper Lane, London EC1A 1BB
  • By phone: +44 20 7123 4567
